Welcome to my new digital garden.

This portfolio is designed to be minimal, content-forward, and fast. I built it using:
- **Next.js 15** with the App Router
- **Tailwind CSS v4** for styling
- **Framer Motion** for subtle scroll animations
- **MDX** for blog posts

## Why this stack?

I wanted a tech stack that I'm comfortable with but also pushes the boundaries of modern web development. The App Router makes data fetching and layouts a breeze, while Tailwind's new v4 engine is incredibly fast.
